# Domain-driven design

1. Collaborate on business language, rules, workflows, exceptions, and sources of truth.
2. Separate bounded contexts where language, models, ownership, or change cadence differ.
3. Model identity with entities and immutable descriptive concepts with value objects.
4. Define aggregate boundaries around invariants and transactional consistency, not object graphs.
5. Publish domain events for meaningful completed facts and handle cross-context effects explicitly.
6. Avoid tactical DDD patterns in simple CRUD domains where they add no value.
