# NestJS

1. Confirm the installed NestJS version, transport, persistence layer, and module conventions.
2. Organize by feature; keep controllers thin and business rules in injectable application services.
3. Use DTO validation and transformation at transport boundaries.
4. Enforce authentication and authorization with guards and resource-level policy checks.
5. Use filters for stable error contracts and interceptors only for genuine cross-cutting behavior.
6. Avoid circular module dependencies and hidden service-locator patterns.
7. Test services in isolation and critical routes with integration tests.
