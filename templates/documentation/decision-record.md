# Architecture decision record

## Status and owner

Record proposed/accepted/superseded status, accountable owner, date, and related issue or change.

## Context and constraints

Describe the concrete problem, affected users, existing architecture, supported environments, and measurable acceptance criteria. Separate observed evidence from assumptions.

## Decision

State the selected approach, ownership boundaries, and why the smallest viable alternative meets the requirements.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Compare realistic alternatives, including retaining the current design. Explain correctness, maintenance, cost, and operational consequences rather than listing buzzwords.

## Security, data, and compatibility

Identify trust boundaries, authorization, sensitive data, concurrent writes, public contracts, migrations, and backward compatibility. State which items are not applicable and why.

## Rollout and recovery

Record deployment order, migration/backfill ownership, rollback or forward-recovery conditions, monitoring, and the person responsible for stopping a failed rollout.

## Validation evidence

List exact checks, outcomes, environment, and links to evidence. Distinguish local validation from live behavior. Mark unavailable checks explicitly; never invent results.

## Remaining work

List unresolved questions with owners and release impact, or state none. Link the superseding decision when this record is replaced.
